# Content drafts

Three fields per row, in the order a reader meets them:

1. **blurb** - the two-line card text in the grid. Clamped to 2 lines, about
   160 characters at the card's font size. Longer text is cut mid-sentence.
2. **tldr** - the TL;DR callout at the top of the detail page. Longer than the
   blurb, since a reader who clicked through wants more than the card gave
   them. If it matches the blurb the callout is hidden, so it earns its place
   only by saying something new.
3. **content** - the body, in STAR order.

STAR maps onto the structure the eight project write-ups already use:

    Situation   the problem, and who it hurt
    Task        what you set out to do, and your role
    Action      what you actually built or led
    Result      what came of it

The projects already have Problem / Solution / My Contributions, which is STAR
with Result missing and the first two headings renamed. The achievements have
no sections at all: most are one to three sentences, so their content is
written from scratch rather than restructured.

t4g-yourmedi is done and live as a worked example of the target shape.

Fill in what you want changed and hand the file back. Anything left blank stays
as it is. Result is the field I cannot draft: it needs real outcomes, so if a
row has none worth stating, write "none" and I will leave the section out
rather than pad it.
